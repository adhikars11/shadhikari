I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of California, Davis. My research lies at the intersection of macroeconomics, labor markets, and climate change, with a focus on structural models, time series forecasting, and the transition policies. I am broadly interested in applying data science and economic theory to real-world challenges.

I bring a versatile background spanning academia, public policy, and tech. I previously interned as an Economist at the International Monetary Fund and as a Software Development Intern and Data Science Educator at Posit (formerly RStudio). My past work includes predictive emissions modeling, labor market frictions, and building scalable data tools to support product development and user education.

I am currently seeking full-time roles starting in 2026.
