I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of California, Davis, working on the topic of macroeconomics, labor markets, and climate change. My research combines structural modeling, time-series forecasting, and policy analysis to study transition dynamics in the economy. Broadly, I apply data science and economic theory to answer policy-oriented questions.

My experience spans academia, public policy, and technology. I have served as an Economist Intern at the International Monetary Fund and as a Software Development Intern and Data Science Educator at Posit (formerly RStudio). My past work includes predictive emissions modeling, labor market frictions, and building scalable data tools to support product development and user education.

I am currently seeking full-time roles starting in 2026.
